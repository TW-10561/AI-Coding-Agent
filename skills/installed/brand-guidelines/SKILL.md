## Best Practices

1. **Keep guidelines accessible** to all team members
2. **Update regularly** with new applications
3. **Include visual examples** of correct and incorrect usage
4. **Make it practical** with real-world scenarios
5. **Ensure consistency** across all touchpoints
6. **Get feedback** from stakeholders
7. **Version your guidelines**
8. **Provide templates** when possible
9. **Include rationale** for guidelines
10. **Review compliance** regularly
