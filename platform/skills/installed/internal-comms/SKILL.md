## Best Practices

### Frequency & Consistency
- Regular communication schedule
- Predictable channels
- Consistent tone and style
- Reliable delivery

### Clarity & Transparency
- Clear, simple language
- Avoid jargon
- Transparent about challenges
- Explain decisions
- Share rationale

### Two-Way Communication
- Solicit feedback
- Listen actively
- Respond to concerns
- Act on suggestions
- Close feedback loops

### Inclusivity
- Consider all employees
- Multiple language support
- Accessibility compliance
- Time zones
- Different communication preferences

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Broadcasting without listening**
2. **Inconsistent messaging** across channels
3. **Silence during uncertain** times
4. **Overcommunicating** on trivial matters
5. **Ignoring feedback**
6. **Using only one** communication channel
7. **Leadership isolation** from staff
8. **Lack of authenticity**
9. **No clear call-to-action**
10. **Poor email** etiquette/overload
